## What is Ticketer Pro?

Ticketer Pro enhances your existing setup with additional features, higher limits, and more control over how your tickets work.

It builds on top of the free plan — you don’t lose anything, you just unlock more.

## What does Pro include?

With Ticketer Pro, you get access to:

- Higher limits for Panels, Tickets, and overall usage
- Advanced access to Ticketer AI and Ticketer Cloud
- Advanced configuration options for more complex setups
- More advanced features (branding, estimated wait time calculations, advanced insights/analytics and more)
- Faster and more direct support

Pro is built for servers that want more than just the basics.

## How does billing work?

Ticketer Pro is a subscription that is billed per server (guild).

- You activate Pro for a specific server
- Billing is handled securely through Stripe, either as monthly or annual plan
- Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled

You can manage your subscription at any time through the dashboard.

## Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time via the dashboard.\
Your Pro features will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

## When should I upgrade?

You should consider upgrading to Pro if:

- Your server is growing and hitting limits
- You need more advanced configuration options
- You want faster support and early feature access
- You rely heavily on tickets for your community or business

## How do I upgrade?

To upgrade to Ticketer Pro:

1. Go to the Dashboard
2. Select your server
3. Click on "Upgrade to Pro"
4. Complete checkout

Once completed, Pro will be activated instantly for your server.

You can also access the subscriptions via [https://ticketer.bot/pricing](https://ticketer.bot/pricing).
